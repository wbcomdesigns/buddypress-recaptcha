# Introduction

> **Included in Free**

Wbcom CAPTCHA Manager puts every CAPTCHA on your site behind one settings screen. You pick one provider, add its keys, and switch protection on per form.

## What it protects

- WordPress login, registration, lost password and comments.
- WooCommerce login, registration, lost password, product reviews and checkout (block and classic).
- BuddyPress member registration, group creation and the Contact Me form, and bbPress topics and replies.
- Contact Form 7, WPForms, Gravity Forms, Ninja Forms, Forminator, Elementor Pro Forms and Divi contact forms.
- Easy Digital Downloads, MemberPress and Ultimate Member forms.
- A login widget and a login block, both protected.

A setting for a plugin only appears when that plugin is active.

## Requirements

WordPress 6.5 or higher and PHP 8.1 or higher. BuddyPress, WooCommerce and bbPress are optional.
