# FAQ

**Which provider should I use?**
Use ALTCHA if you want no third-party service. Otherwise Turnstile or hCaptcha are the least intrusive checkboxes.

**Why does no CAPTCHA show?**
The keys are not saved, the form's switch is off, or your IP is on the whitelist.

**Does it work with BuddyBoss?**
Yes. BuddyPress forms and the login widget work on BuddyBoss Platform.

**Can I use two providers?**
No. One provider is active at a time.

**Does it slow my pages?**
Its scripts load only on pages that show a CAPTCHA.

**Is Gravity Forms or MemberPress covered?**
Yes, both are supported.
